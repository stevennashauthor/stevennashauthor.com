About the author

Steven Nash

Steven Nash writes speculative fiction about the systems that shape us — and what they remember, erase, and refuse to let go. His work spans the six-volume Falling Moon series, the five-volume Rustmour Chronicles, and ten standalone novels of suspense, identity, and dangerous certainty.

He lives in Maine, where he writes about institutional failure and the people caught inside it.
